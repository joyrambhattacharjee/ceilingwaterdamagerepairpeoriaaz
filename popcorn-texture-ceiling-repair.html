Texture Types

Matching every ceiling finish

Peoria homes have a range of ceiling finishes, and each is matched a little differently. Popcorn (acoustic) texture is sprayed to match spatter size and density. Knockdown is sprayed then flattened with a knife for a mottled look. Orange-peel is a finer spray texture. Smooth ceilings demand the most careful drywall finishing because there is no texture to hide imperfections. Whatever your ceiling, the goal is the same: a repaired area that is indistinguishable from the rest once painted.

FAQ

Popcorn & texture repair questions

Can you match my popcorn ceiling?

Yes — matching popcorn and other textures is a core part of what we do, so the repaired area blends with the rest of the ceiling.

Should I remove popcorn while repairing?

It is a good opportunity if you would like a more modern look. We can repair-and-match or remove-and-refinish, whichever you prefer.

Will the old water stain show through the new texture?

Not when it is sealed properly. We use a stain-blocking primer before repainting so previous stains do not bleed back through.

Is my popcorn ceiling safe to repair?

If your home predates the mid-1980s, we recommend asbestos testing before disturbing the texture. Intact texture is low-risk; the hazard comes from scraping or sanding it untested.

Can you match knockdown or orange-peel texture too?

Yes. We match knockdown, orange-peel, smooth and popcorn finishes so the repair is seamless.

Do you paint the whole ceiling or just the patch?

For the most even result we recommend painting the whole ceiling, since textured surfaces reflect light unevenly and spot-painting can show.

How long does a texture repair take?

Once the ceiling is dry and the drywall patched, texturing, priming and painting typically takes a day or two with dry time between steps.

Matching Technique

How we make texture disappear

Matching texture is part science, part craft. More Questions

Popcorn & texture repair, in depth

Can you remove popcorn entirely instead of matching it?

Yes — many homeowners use water damage as a chance to go smooth or knockdown. We can match or modernize, whichever you prefer.

Why does popcorn texture fall off after a leak?

Water softens the texture and the bond to the drywall, so it loosens and flakes. The damaged area is scraped and rebuilt to match.

Will the new texture match perfectly?

We match spatter size and density closely and test before applying, so the repaired area blends with the surrounding ceiling once painted.

Is my older popcorn ceiling safe to disturb?

If the home predates the mid-1980s, we recommend asbestos testing before disturbing it. Intact texture is low-risk; scraping untested texture is not.

Do you paint just the patch or the whole ceiling?

The whole ceiling gives the most seamless result, since textured surfaces catch light unevenly and spot-painting tends to show.
