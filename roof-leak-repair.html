What We Do

Stop the leak, then fix the damage

Roof leaks are the most common cause of ceiling water stains in Peoria. Cracked tiles, worn underlayment, failed flashing around vents and skylights, and wind-driven monsoon rain can all let water track along the roof deck and drip onto your ceiling — often far from the actual entry point.

We locate the true source, help coordinate the roof repair, then dry the attic cavity and restore the ceiling below. Know The Signs

Is it really a roof leak?

A few clues point to the roof as the source. A stain that grows or reappears after rain is the clearest sign. Staining near the roofline, around a skylight, or beneath a roof penetration (vent, pipe or chimney) often traces to failed flashing. Multiple stains appearing after a single storm suggest wind-driven intrusion. Our guide to the warning signs of ceiling water damage covers how to read these clues. The important thing is not to simply repaint — without stopping the leak, the stain returns, and the hidden moisture can grow mold in the attic above.

FAQ

Roof leak ceiling repair questions

The stain only appears when it rains — is that a roof leak?

Almost certainly. A stain that grows or reappears after rain points strongly to water entering through the roof. We trace it to the source so it can be sealed.

Do you repair the roof too?

We focus on finding the source and restoring the interior ceiling, and we coordinate the roofing repair so the whole problem is solved, not just the symptom.

Is monsoon roof-leak damage covered by insurance?

Sudden, storm-driven leaks are frequently covered, while damage from long-neglected maintenance may not be. We document the damage so you can file with confidence; confirm specifics with your insurer.

How do you find a leak that isn’t obvious?

We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water back from the interior stain, since water often travels along the roof deck before it drips.

Can a small roof leak cause big ceiling damage?

Yes. Even a minor gap can admit a surprising amount of water during a storm, saturating insulation and drywall and leading to sagging or mold if left unaddressed.

Will the repaired ceiling match the rest of the room?

Yes — we match the texture and repaint so the repair blends in. Painting the whole ceiling gives the most uniform finish.

How soon should I act on a roof-leak stain?

As soon as possible. The longer water sits in the attic and ceiling, the more it spreads and the higher the mold risk, so prompt action keeps the repair smaller.

Can you tell where the leak is from inside?

How long until I should fix a roof-leak stain?

Quickly — every rain adds more water, and lingering moisture in the attic can grow mold. Prompt action keeps the repair small.
